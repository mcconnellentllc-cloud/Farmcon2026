⛽ E15 Ethanol: Closer Than Ever

A potential game-changer for corn demand

The Big News from FARM Con

"They are closer than ever to get E15 ethanol mandated."

This would be a significant win for corn producers - moving from E10 (10% ethanol) to E15 (15% ethanol) nationwide would increase corn demand substantially.

Why E15 Matters for Our Farm

📈 Increased Corn Demand

A move from E10 to E15 represents a 50% increase in ethanol blend rates.

  • Roughly 700 million+ additional bushels of corn demand
  • Stronger basis at local ethanol plants
  • More marketing options for corn producers
  • Price support through increased demand

💰 Price Support

More demand = better prices, especially important given the cautious outlook on other commodities.

  • Offsets some bearish pressure on corn
  • Creates floor under prices
  • Regional benefits near ethanol plants
  • Potential for expansion of ethanol capacity

🌽 Market Diversification

With soybean prices potentially going sub-$10, stronger corn markets help diversify risk.

  • Corn becomes more attractive vs beans
  • May influence planting decisions
  • Helps balance rotation economics
  • Reduces dependence on export markets

🏭 Local Economic Impact

More ethanol production means more economic activity in rural communities.

  • Ethanol plant investments
  • DDGs production for livestock
  • Local jobs and infrastructure
  • Corn oil production opportunities

What's Happening Politically

Current Status

The Big Beautiful Bill and other legislative efforts are pushing for nationwide E15 adoption.

  • Year-Round E15 Sales Removing summer volatility restrictions that limit E15 sales in warm months
  • Infrastructure Investments Funding for pumps and tanks that can handle higher ethanol blends
  • RFS Support Maintaining and strengthening Renewable Fuel Standard requirements
  • Vehicle Compatibility Ensuring automakers continue to support E15+ capable vehicles

What This Means for Planning

Planting Decisions

If E15 mandate passes, corn acres become more attractive. Factor this into crop mix decisions, but don't bet the farm on it passing.

Planning

Marketing Strategy

Relationships with local ethanol plants may become more valuable. Consider basis contracts and delivery agreements.

Marketing

Risk Balance

E15 corn demand helps offset some risk from weak soybean prices. Consider the combined risk profile of your rotation.

Risk Management

Questions to Track

What We Need to Monitor

  • Timeline: When might E15 mandate actually happen?
  • What's the legislative path - part of Big Beautiful Bill or separate?
  • How quickly would demand increase once passed?
  • Are local ethanol plants planning expansion?
  • How does our local basis compare to other areas near ethanol plants?
  • What's the oil industry's opposition strategy?

Connecting the Dots

The Bigger Picture

E15 is part of a broader story:

  • Soybeans sub-$10? → Corn becomes relatively more attractive
  • E15 mandate? → Corn demand increases significantly
  • Traceability value? → Low-carbon corn could get premiums for ethanol
  • Big Beautiful Bill? → Supporting infrastructure and mandates

These pieces fit together. Understanding each helps make better farm decisions.
