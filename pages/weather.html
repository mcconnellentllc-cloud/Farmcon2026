The Two Main Phases of This Year's Cycling Weather Pattern

Phase 1: Systems Tap Into Arctic Air

  • Now through third week of January
  • Second half of February
  • Mid-March – Early April
  • Late April to mid-May

Arctic blasts, snow, winter storms in upper midwest/northeast. Severe weather in southern plains.

Phase 2: West Coast Storm Tracks

  • January 25 – February 10
  • First half of March (Return of big CA storm)
  • Early April – Late April
  • Late May – Mid-June

Stormy & very wet West Coast. Cold retreats, warmer & drier conditions east.

FORECAST: Major Mid-Summer Heat Wave

Target Date: August 1, 2026 (Late July – Early August)

Center of the Major Heat Wave:

Central Plains (Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas panhandle region)

Heat expands outward across most of the continental US

Heat builds 10 weeks before peak: Expect warming trend starting mid to late May

Plan accordingly for livestock, crops, and operations.
