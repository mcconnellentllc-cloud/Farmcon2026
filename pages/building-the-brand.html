Building the Brand

Farm Con 2026 - Day 2 | Stand Out. Sell. Automate.

Farmers Need to Build a Brand to Stand Out

Sell and Automate

It Also Means You Have to Listen to Your Customer

NOT JUST SAY YOU'RE LISTENING TO YOUR CUSTOMER

Actually hear what they need. Respond to it. Build your brand around solving their real problems.

Think of Your Customer AS A FAN

Not just a buyer. A fan. Someone who believes in what you do, tells others about you, and keeps coming back.

What's Changed Is How We Need to Think and Act

TO MOTIVATE FANS TO WANT TO BE SEEN AND BELONG WITH US

People don't just want to buy - they want to belong to something. Make them proud to be associated with your brand.

Don't Worry About the Marketing Department

Word of mouth is still the #1 form of marketing.

Make people become YOUR marketing department.

When fans love what you do, they sell for you - for free.

Lessons From: Barstool Sports

  • Love your customer - don't market to your marketing department
  • Super serve them on their terms, on their timeline, and in the places where they live

Meet your customers where they are, not where you want them to be.

Be Consistent and Be Constant

Your brand is built over time through repeated, reliable actions. Show up the same way, every time. That's how trust is built.

You Don't Have to Perfect a Message or Content Clip

IT'S A VOLUME GAME

Don't wait for perfection. Put content out there. The more you create, the more chances you have to connect.
