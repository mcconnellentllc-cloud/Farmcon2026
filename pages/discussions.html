Risk Management Strategy: Given Sub-$10 Beans

Priority: HIGH | Status: Active Discussion

With the projection of soybeans potentially going below $10, how should we adjust our risk management approach?

Key Points to Discuss:
  • What coverage level makes sense for crop insurance? (75%? 80%? 85%?)
  • Should we be forward contracting more aggressively?
  • How do ARC and PLC elections factor in?
  • What's our break-even cost per bushel?
Next Steps: Calculate actual break-even costs, compare insurance premium costs at different levels, model scenarios.

Implementing Big Beautiful Bill Programs

Priority: HIGH | Status: Research Needed

We know the bill is good for farmers - now we need to make sure we're taking full advantage.

Questions to Answer:
  • Which programs apply to our specific operation?
  • What are the signup deadlines we can't miss?
  • Who do we need to talk to? (FSA, NRCS, insurance agent, accountant)
  • Are there programs we've been missing out on?
Next Steps: Schedule meetings with FSA and NRCS. See our Farm Bill Benefits page for detailed breakdown.

"We're Not the Low Cost Producer" - Implications

Priority: HIGH | Status: Strategic Discussion

This reality check has big implications for how we run our operation.

  • What role does technology play in our competitiveness?
  • Should we be looking at different crops or enterprises?
