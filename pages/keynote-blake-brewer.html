Blake Brewer - The Legacy Letter

Blake Brewer

Founder, Legacy Letter Challenge | Speaker | Coach

At 19, Blake was snorkeling with his dad in Hawaii when tragedy struck - his father drowned. Hours later, his mom found a letter his dad had written to him. That letter changed everything. It gave Blake hope, direction, and a mission.

His father Larry Brewer was Terry Bradshaw's tight end at Louisiana Tech, drafted by the Atlanta Falcons, a hospital CEO, and a Sunday School teacher. But to Blake, he was just Dad - his hero.

Now Blake is on a mission to help 1 million parents write at least one meaningful, lasting Legacy Letter to their children.
