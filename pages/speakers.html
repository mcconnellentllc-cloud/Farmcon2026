Zack Kass

AI Futurist | Former Head of Go-to-Market at OpenAI

Session: Wednesday, 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

About Zack

Zack Kass is an AI Futurist, keynote speaker, consultant, and the former Head of Go-to-Market for OpenAI, where he built the company's sales, solutions, and partnerships teams. With over 14 years in AI, he has led large teams at some of the industry's most important companies including Figure Eight, Lilt, and OpenAI.

At OpenAI, Zack led the teams responsible for sales, partnerships, and customer success, turning the company's cutting-edge research into real-world business solutions. Today, he advises Fortune 1000 companies and governments on how to lead in an AI-driven world.

As an executive business advisor, Zack works with Fortune 1,000 boardrooms and leadership teams - including Coca-Cola, Morgan Stanley, and Amgen - to help leaders navigate the rapidly evolving AI landscape.

Zack is a staunch techno-optimist and advocate for AI's potential to revolutionize the human condition and reduce suffering. His thought leadership has been featured in Fortune, Newsweek, Entrepreneur, AdAge, and Business Insider.

Academic Roles

Zack collaborates with the University of Virginia to explore AI's socio-economic impacts. He was also appointed Distinguished Adjunct Professor at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia's Faculty of Artificial Intelligence.

Blake Brewer

CEO & Founder, Legacy Letter Challenge | Speaker | Author

Session: Wednesday, 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM

About Blake

Blake Brewer is on a mission to help 1 million parents write at least one well-written, meaningful, lasting Legacy Letter to their children. He is the CEO and founder of The Endurance and the Legacy Letter Challenge.

His Story

The Legacy Letter Challenge began with a tragedy that became a gift. When Blake was 19 years old, his family went on vacation to Hawaii. On the first day, he and his dad were out snorkeling when his father drowned.

That evening, Blake received a letter from his father - those would be the final words he'd ever receive from him. His father had been writing him a letter for several months and was planning to give it to him on the trip. On a simple sheet of paper, his father shared his thoughts, wisdom, encouragement, and advice.

18 years later, these words continue to guide Blake through life. This powerful experience inspired him to help other parents leave the same gift for their children - without waiting for tragedy to strike.

His Mission

Blake travels the country leading workshops on writing legacy letters and delivering keynote addresses. The Legacy Letter Challenge has already positively elevated the relationships of tens of thousands of children with their parents, healing broken relationships and strengthening good ones.

Free Resources

A free toolkit for people to download and start working on their own Legacy Letters is available at LegacyLetter.com.

Erika Ayers Badan

CEO of Food52 | Former CEO of Barstool Sports | Author

Session: Thursday, 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

About Erika

Erika Ayers Badan is an American businesswoman, currently serving as CEO of Food52. She was the CEO of Barstool Sports from 2016 to 2024, where she oversaw extraordinary growth.

Barstool Sports Success

Under her leadership, Barstool Sports' $15 million valuation in 2016 grew to $30 million after just one year, and then to $450 million in 2020. As of her resignation in January 2024, the company's revenue had grown 5,000% since she first assumed the role.

During her tenure, Barstool became a top ten podcasting publisher in the US, with the world's #1 sports, hockey, golf, and music podcasts, and a top 6 brand globally on TikTok. She launched over 35 brands, including breakout franchises like Pat McAfee, Bussin with the Boys, and Call Her Daddy.
