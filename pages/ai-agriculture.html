AI in Agriculture: Session Summary

Key takeaways from Zack Kass - AI Futurist, Former Head of Go-to-Market at OpenAI

"Today is the best day ever to be born."

"Optimism is not naive."

— Zack Kass | zackkass.com

The Big Ideas

Unmetered Intelligence

AI cost has collapsed: GPT-4 went from $60 to $1.30 per million tokens. Intelligence is becoming unlimited and nearly free.

For farmers: The analytical power big corporations had is now accessible to everyone.

Who Uses It Wins

"How and who chooses to use it will define brilliance."

The advantage goes to those who actually try it, ask better questions, and start NOW. Early adopters compound their learning.

Expand Human Potential

"The amount we can do today is astronomical vs the 1800s."

Each technology generation compounds - tractors, GPS, precision ag, now AI. What could YOUR farm accomplish with unlimited analytical capacity?

Supercharge Problem Solvers

"AI will supercharge those trying to solve very complex problems."

Not just simple tasks - AI opens doors to challenges that were previously too complex to attempt.

Four Risks to Watch

1. Idiocracy

If AI does all our thinking, we lose critical thinking. Use it or lose it.

2. Dehumanization

Risk of losing what makes us human - connection, judgment, meaning.

3. Bad Actors

AI amplifies capability for scams, fraud, manipulation - stay skeptical.

4. Job Displacement

Entire job categories may disappear. Adapt or be left behind.

The balance: Use AI as a tool, not a replacement. Stay engaged. Keep learning.

The Farmer's Reality Check

Zack mentioned "deflation" from AI efficiency...

Has this guy been in a grocery store lately? Input costs UP. Labor UP. Land UP. What farmers get paid? FLAT.

Take "AI will make everything cheaper" with a grain of salt. Farm economics don't always follow tech predictions.

The Promise: More Time

"Quality of life and standard of life will get better. We will get more time back."

What To Do With That Time

"Spend time with family and friends. Anchor your mission, vision, values. You have to be principled."

When AI handles busy work, invest freed time in what matters: family, purpose, legacy.

The WHY/HOW Framework

"What is your WHY?"

Your purpose doesn't change with technology. Why do you farm? What drives you?

The WHY stays constant.

"Be adaptable to your HOW"

Methods, tools, processes - these SHOULD change as better options emerge.

The HOW must evolve.

Your grandfather's WHY still applies. His HOW needs to evolve. Don't confuse the two.

"Learn How to Learn"

Perhaps the most important advice: when technology changes constantly, the most valuable skill is learning new things quickly.

  • Stay curious - try one new AI tool this month
  • Be willing to be bad at first
  • Model learning for the next generation

"Be Human"

Zack's closing message: In an age of artificial intelligence, the most valuable thing you can be is authentically human.

What AI can't replace: Relationships, judgment, creativity, purpose, connection.

"Don't let the digital world create the hate." Prioritize face-to-face over online arguments. Build community in the physical world.

Use AI to handle the artificial. Invest yourself in the human.

"Optimism is a Moral Obligation"

But grounded in action, not wishful thinking:

  1. Describe the problems - don't ignore challenges, name them clearly
  2. Give solutions - every problem needs an answer

Pessimism is easy. Optimism paired with action is how progress happens.

For farmers: You've always been optimists. Every seed planted is an act of faith in the future.

Your Action Items

  • Start today - the farmer who starts NOW has the advantage
  • Pick ONE thing - what task eats your time but doesn't need YOUR brain?
  • Learn how to learn - try one new AI tool this month
  • Document your rules - write down your decision processes (they become AI instructions)
  • Stay human - use AI for the artificial, invest yourself in relationships and purpose
